## 2. Scope in one paragraph

Furnish and install two 3,400 kWe, 4,160 V trailer-mounted liquid-fuel mobile gas turbine generators, one at Substation #1 and one at Substation #6. Each connects through a new 1,200 A generator breaker with bus differential in the existing 4.16 kV switchgear, a new above-ground quick-connect junction box, and metal-conduit power and control cabling. A DEF system, Modbus TCP SCADA integration under RMF, CO2 fire suppression, and an extensive factory and site test program are also required. BFA designs the site and interconnection, writes the specifications, and approves the OEM package as DOR.

## 5. Highest-priority RFP conflicts (submit as PPIs)

1. **PPI-11 — Nuclear Class 1E criteria** (IEEE 308/323/387, NQA-1, radiation dose, ECCS) carried into a mobile GTG spec. Cannot be priced if enforced.
2. **PPI-07 — DEF / 'Tier IV' on a gas turbine.** Implies SCR; NOx limit and permittee undefined.
3. **PPI-01 — Substations #1A and #4** appear in the description but not in the scope or drawings.
4. **PPI-15 — Sub #6 has no shown spare cubicle** for the new generator breaker and 87B.
5. **PPI-08 / PPI-09 — Fuel and trailer math:** 4,400 gal ÷ 350 gal/h = 12.6 h (14.6 h required); the E-107 trailer is ≈52 ft against a 48 ft limit.
6. **PPI-03 — Proprietary indicators** (SoLoNOx, ES 9-98) and a single- vs split-shaft conflict.
7. **PPI-16 / PPI-19 — Grid-parallel operation and system grounding** (4-wire MGN wye vs HRG/NGR).
