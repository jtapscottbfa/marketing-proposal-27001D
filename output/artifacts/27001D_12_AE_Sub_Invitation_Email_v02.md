## Project at a glance

- **Project:** FY27 CIP MINCON Substation Generators, Naval Support Facility Dahlgren, VA (NAVFAC Washington, Project 1921806)
- **Solicitation:** N4008027R0004. Design-build, price-only award.
- **Team:** Encon Corporation is the design-build offeror. BFA is the prime A/E and Designer of Record, and self-performs electrical, mechanical and plumbing.
- **Work:** Furnish and install two trailer-mounted 3,400 kWe, 4,160 V liquid-fuel (No. 2 oil) gas turbine generators. One goes at Substation #1 (on asphalt, open site) and one at Substation #6 (existing concrete pad, fenced). The work includes:
  - quick-connect power and control interfaces
  - new generator breakers and bus differential protection in the existing 4.16 kV switchgear
  - DEIF load-management and load-shedding controls
  - a Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) system
  - on-trailer fuel tanks (8 × 550 gal per unit)
  - an NFPA 12 CO2 enclosure fire-suppression system
- **Excluded from A/E scope:** The internal design of the generator package (turbine, generator, enclosure, CO2, fire and gas detection, on-board controls) is by the OEM. BFA specifies it, integrates it and approves it as Designer of Record.
- **Contract duration:** 24 months from award. There is no building on this project.

## Your scope by discipline

**Civil**

- Site plans for both substations.
- Grading and drainage, limit of work, and laydown and staging.
- Trailer delivery access and turning paths for a 48–52 ft drop-deck trailer.
- Pavement repair at Substation #1.
- Erosion and sediment control and Chesapeake Bay Preservation Act compliance (Part 3 G10/G1030; Part 4 G10).

**Structural**

- Trailer and fuel-tank pad design at both sites, with jacking-leg bearing (Part 3 1.18.1.1.1).
- Tie-downs and anchorage for 120 mph wind and Central Virginia Seismic Zone criteria (Part 3 1.7.19, 1.11.17).
- Foundations for the junction box and DEF tank.
- Conduit and cable supports, and bollards.
- Evaluate the existing concrete pad at Substation #6 for reuse.

**Geotechnical**

- Borings at both pad locations.
- Bearing capacity, pavement section and groundwater.
- A sealed geotechnical report (a DOR-approved submittal; Part 4 G10.3, 01 33 00 1.1.a.2).

**Land Surveying / SUE**

- Topographic survey of both substation sites (required for the design drawings, Part 3 G10).
- Utility designation (SUE Quality Level B) with verification at conflict points along the cable and conduit routes.

**Fire Protection (Qualified Fire Protection Engineer)**

- QFPE services per UFC 3-600-01.
- NFPA 37 separation analysis:
  - Substation #1: Buildings 115 and 248
  - Substation #6: Building 1500
- Review of the OEM's NFPA 12 CO2 system, including the site full-discharge test requirement (Part 3 1.27).
- NFPA 30 review of the fuel tanks.
- Fire protection submittal approval as DOR, and attendance at the NAVFAC Fire Protection Engineer meeting (01 30 00 1.3).

**Controls / Cybersecurity (ICS-RMF)**

- Cybersecurity support for the generator and switchgear control systems per DoDI 8500.01, DoDI 8510.01 (RMF) and NIST SP 800-82 Appendix G (Part 3 2.4):
  - categorization support
  - control implementation
  - network architecture
  - eMASS artifacts
  - hardening of the PLC, HMI and relays
  - Modbus TCP boundary protection for SCADA integration (Part 3 1.21)
- Coordination with BFA on the DEIF AGC 200 and ALC-4 load-shedding controls (Part 3 1.25).

**Environmental / Air Permitting**

- VDEQ air-permit applicability review and application support (NSPS Subpart KKKK, BACT; Part 3 1.3.19, 1.32).
- SPCC (40 CFR 112) and 9VAC25-91 aboveground storage tank compliance.
- The Project Rating, Operating and Discharge (PROD) form (01 30 00 1.6).
- Wetland and Resource Protection Area screening at both sites. Concept drawing E-101 shows a water/wetland area west of Substation #1.

**Acoustical (optional; quote separately)**

- A site noise assessment at the adjacent occupied buildings, using OEM sound data (Part 3 1.31).
